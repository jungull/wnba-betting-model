Evidence notes — what each mark means

¹ Receipted. Walk-forward, five chronological folds, games never split; the strongest evidence class in the program.

² Archive caveat. Market numbers come from the 2022–26 snapshot archive captured Aug 6 — two snapshots per game day with vendor-asserted timestamps. Honest for accuracy-vs-outcome; forbidden for any timing claim.

³ Sealed by design. Challenger results cannot be seen — by us or anyone — until the implementation audit and integrity checks pass. Prevents cherry-picking before it can happen.

⁴ Head-to-head standard. Comparisons only on identical as-of universes: our prediction at time T vs de-vigged market at time T vs outcome. No mixed-timestamp comparisons, ever.

⁵ Threshold-first rule. A single prop line yields a de-vigged over/under probability — the primary quantity. An "implied mean" is only shown where alternate lines exist or a distribution has been calibrated out of sample.

⁶ Source hierarchy. Official quarter-hour injury report > team/league announcement > credentialed reporter > archived projections > news > Wikipedia revision > participation inference.
