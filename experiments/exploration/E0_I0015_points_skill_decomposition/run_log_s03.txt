STEP 3d -- MECHANISM: WHY DO INDIVIDUALLY-BETTER FACTORS MULTIPLY INTO A WORSE PRODUCT?
====================================================================================================

  POINTS = MINUTES x RATE is a PRODUCT, and the conditional mean of a product is
      E[M*R] = E[M]*E[R] + Cov(M, R).
  Multiplying two separately-good marginal forecasts therefore omits the covariance term, and
  amplifies each factor's variance into the other.  Two measurable consequences are checked below.

  (i) realised corr(minutes, points-per-minute):  pooled +0.3506   WITHIN player-season +0.1499
      OBSERVED SIGN IS POSITIVE, so the blowout/garbage-time story (more minutes at LOWER
      efficiency) is NOT what is happening here -- within a player-season, the games where
      a player plays more are also the games she scores at a HIGHER rate.  Cov(M,R) > 0,
      so a product of marginal forecasts UNDERSTATES the mean and the two factors' errors
      reinforce rather than cancel.  Candidate explanation (d) as originally worded is
      REJECTED IN SIGN; the compounding is real but runs the other way.

  (ii) forecast dispersion (sd) against the outcome's own sd:
      y_pts (outcome)                sd= 7.5800  mean bias vs outcome=+0.0000
      REF_pts                        sd= 5.7937  mean bias vs outcome=+0.0364
      H1 model_min x model_rate      sd= 5.7718  mean bias vs outcome=-0.0239
      H2 model_min x naive_rate      sd= 5.8881  mean bias vs outcome=-0.1163
      H3 naive_min x model_rate      sd= 5.7555  mean bias vs outcome=-0.1109
      H4 naive_min x naive_rate      sd= 5.8676  mean bias vs outcome=-0.1494
      A forecast whose sd EXCEEDS what its information supports is over-dispersed and pays
      for it in MAE on a noisy target.  Compare H1 with H3.

  (iii) sd of each factor's own forecast error, and the error correlation that compounds:
      minutes: model sd=6.6362  naive sd=6.8409   (model is the BETTER minutes forecast)
      rate   : model sd=0.24420 naive sd=0.24747  (model is the BETTER rate forecast)
      corr(minutes error, rate error) for model minutes x model rate   = +0.1868
      corr(minutes error, rate error) for naive minutes x model rate   = +0.1529
      corr(minutes error, rate error) for model minutes x naive rate   = +0.1583
      corr(minutes error, rate error) for naive minutes x naive rate   = +0.1572

      corr(champion error, reference error) = +0.9407
      sd(champion forecast)=5.7718  sd(reference)=5.7937  sd(outcome)=7.5800
