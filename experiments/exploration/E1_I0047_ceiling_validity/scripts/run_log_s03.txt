2. ORTHOGONALITY -- CHECKED, NOT ASSUMED
====================================================================================================
  VIF = (sd_x / sd_x_perp)^2 = 1/(1 - R^2 of the candidate on the base).
  VIF = 1 means EXACTLY orthogonal to the base.  C-RAWSD = realised dR2 x VIF.
    min 1.0000000000  p05 1.000000  median 1.003017  p95 1.166666  max 1.678582
    cells with VIF < 1 (the ONLY way C-RAWSD could fail): 0
    cells 'effectively orthogonal' (VIF < 1.01)         : 140 of 213  (65.7%)

  The brief's suspicion was that non-orthogonality breaks the bound. It is the reverse:
  orthogonality is the ZERO-SLACK case (C-RAWSD = realised exactly) and non-orthogonality
  only ADDS slack. There is no configuration of the candidate-base correlation that makes
  C-RAWSD fall below the realised increment for this fit.

7. THE FINDING THAT WEAKENS THE FAVOURABLE VERDICT -- what these kills actually ARE
====================================================================================================
  C-RAWSD is computed FROM beta_hat, the fitted coefficient of the SAME in-sample OLS fit
  whose increment it is supposed to bound. So D097 did not close these channels WITHOUT
  fitting: every one of the 213 was fitted, and the 'ceiling' is that fit's own realised
  increment multiplied by its VIF. The kills are sound. They are not, however, the
  pre-fit arithmetic screen the ruling describes, and they are not fit-invariant.

  CONSEQUENCE FOR E1_I0036's EXCLUSION. That screen excluded all 213 from re-levelling on
  the stated ground 'a ceiling kill is arithmetic and survives re-levelling' (T1_not_ceiling).
  A beta_hat-derived ceiling is NOT invariant to re-levelling: beta_hat, sd_x, sd_y and SST
  all change when player-games are aggregated to team-games.
    of the 213, level is roster-constant in            : 171
    target is summable in                              : 213
    WOULD HAVE BEEN T2 AND T3 ELIGIBLE but for T1      : 171
    E1_I0036 published eligibility                     : 118 of 1580 killed cells (7.5%)
    with these added                                   : 289 of 1580 (18.3%)
  That does not resurrect any of them -- E1_I0036's own arithmetic (detection floor rises
  8.3-9.3x at team level against a ~9.4x dilution gain) says re-levelling roughly cancels,
  and all 213 sit below the PLAYER-level floor before any of that. But the STATED GROUND for
  the exclusion is wrong for this construction, and it is recorded as a defect.

  COMPOSITION NOTE (recorded columns, no name parsing): 20 of the 213 have ceiling exactly 0
  and realised exactly 0 -- they are the no-op placebo, a negative control. A further 20
  are the pure-noise control. So 40 of the 213 'kills' are controls that were never candidates.
