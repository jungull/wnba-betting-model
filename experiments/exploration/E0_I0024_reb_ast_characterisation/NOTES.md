## 1. THE ANSWER, IN THREE LINES

1. **Neither target is more predictable than points.** On the decision stratum, the share of
   variance irreducible even to an oracle knowing the player's whole season *and* their realised
   minutes is **dreb 51.69%, assists 50.82%, points 51.68%** — the same number three times — with
   **oreb at 64.60%**, the least predictable target measured anywhere in this programme.
2. **The reachable headroom is SMALLER, not larger.** Above the best honest prior-history baseline:
   **assists +0.082 R², total rebounds +0.102 R², points +0.161 R².**
3. **Neither evidenced upstream signal delivers.** Shot location → rebounds is dead (0 of 116
   rebound cells survive; best family-wise p 0.3228). Teammate availability → assists survives the
   screen but the mechanism test disqualifies it: it is D089's minutes/opportunity channel, not a
   playmaking channel.

## 3. WHERE I COULD HAVE CHEATED — DISCLOSURE

Each item is something that would have produced a more impressive result, why it would have been
wrong, and what was actually done.

1. **Use realised minutes as a feature.** This is the single biggest lever available: the ladder
   shows it is worth +0.10 to +0.16 R² on every target. It is an outcome. It appears **only** in
   labelled ORACLE rungs and in the M1 mechanism test, never in a candidate or a base.
2. **Build the tip-time teammate variant.** `T01_c04_tiptime` is the strongest version of the
   assist candidate and D089 measured it at roughly double `P01`'s effect. It reads `minutes > 0` in
   *today's* box — a post-game observation. **It was never built.** Probe P4c goes further and
   proves the shipped `A01` is *distinguishable from* a today's-box quantity on 177 of 250 sampled
   rows, so this is demonstrated, not merely asserted.
3. **Open `data/w1_truth/player_game_availability.csv` / `roster_asof.csv`.** These are the first
   files an availability screen reaches for, and they are artifact-granular with
   `fit_through_season: 2026` — filtering does not help. **Not opened.** Availability was rebuilt
   from box membership, the D076 method.
4. **Open `data/zone_maps/*`.** Pre-aggregated and would have saved the whole shotchart pipeline.
   Artifact-granular. **Not opened.** Zones were derived from raw per-shot `SHOT_ZONE_BASIC`.
5. **Report the row-level p.** It is available in every row of `upstream_signals.csv` and it is far
   more flattering: **41 of 210 cells** would flip from null to "significant at 0.05". The
   row-level null is reported **for inflation only** and is never a verdict.
6. **Report `R08_player_ra_share` as a rebound survivor.** This is the most tempting number in the
   screen: dR² 6.49e-03 on offensive rebounds — the largest raw increment anywhere here — with
   row-level p 0.0017 *and* entity-swap p 0.0017. It dies only under the **cyclic-shift** null
   (p 0.9967), exactly D093's trap. Taking the **less** significant of the two entity-level nulls
   is what kills it, and that rule was fixed in the preregistration before any statistic was
   computed.
7. **Screen against `B_SINGLE` only.** With an incomplete reference several rebound candidates look
   much better (e.g. `R02` on dreb, DECISION: p_row 0.0100). D087 named reference incompleteness the
   top-ranked explanation for this programme's nulls, so **the verdict is taken from `B_COMPLETE`**,
   with a further `B_COMPLETE_PLUS_R10` variant that also contains the closest prior *opponent*
   measurement of the target.
8. **Drop the specificity cross-tests.** Putting `A01` on the rebound targets is what destroyed the
   assist story. Those cells were in the **hashed** preregistered list precisely so they could not
   be dropped after seeing the result.
9. **Quote POOLED and suppress DECISION.** POOLED has 2.8× the rows and much better ceilings (A04
   2.884e-03 = 1.40× the programme's largest measured, versus 0.83× on DECISION). POOLED includes
   cold-start and low-minute rows where the teammate channel is mostly predicting *who plays*. Both
   strata are reported side by side throughout, and the decision-relevant reading is DECISION.
10. **Quote the in-sample dR² and skip propagation.** The walk-forward test reduces the surviving
    assist signal to **+0.066% MAE skill**. It is reported.
11. **Skip the per-minute test.** It is the test that most cleanly kills the assist interpretation
    (p 0.49–0.96, sign flipping between strata). It is reported.
12. **Tune the history minutes floor.** Floor choice moves the rate references materially. It was
    **preregistered at 10 minutes** with a declared sensitivity ladder, not chosen after seeing
    results.
13. **Quietly fix the vacuous leakage probe.** s02's probe P4 asserted nothing and printed `[PASS]`.
    It is recorded in `DEFECTS.md` as D-01, the original row is retained in `leakage_probes.csv`
    **relabelled `P4_VACUOUS_SUPERSEDED` and flipped to `passed=False`**, and the replacement probes

14. **Present the post-hoc points test as preregistered.** A01/A04 on points was added in s05. It is
    declared, counted against the hash, and flagged — it *strengthened* the null rather than

## 4. THE MINUTES FLOOR — HISTORY ONLY

D093 measured that a realised-minutes floor removes 39.3% of per-minute variance, and per-minute
rates computed on four-minute appearances are noise. That is a **measurement**, not a licence to
filter the response: filtering the response conditions on an outcome (D091 ruling 3).

- **Applied to:** which prior games contribute to a per-minute rate estimate. Floor = 10 min.
- **Never applied to:** the response. All 18,212 appeared rows are retained and scored.
- Measured on this frame (`history_floor_curve.csv`): at a floor of 20, history variance falls
  **36.1%** for rebounds-per-minute and **32.5%** for assists-per-minute — close to D093's 39.3% on
  points-per-minute, so the same phenomenon is present in these targets.
- The floored rate is combined with an **unconditional** prior mean minutes, because a real forecast
  must predict minutes over the whole distribution, not just the games above the floor.

## 6. FLOOR OF RESOLUTION

The no-op placebo (an exact affine copy of the base's first column) returns dR² **exactly 0.000** on
all 20 control cells, as it must. Its observed null SD is the resolution floor of this design:

| stratum | placebo null SD |
|---|---|
| DECISION (n≈5,111) | 1.59e-04 – 2.22e-04 |
| POOLED (n≈14,327) | 4.46e-05 – 7.53e-05 |

**Any increment below that floor is unresolvable by this design regardless of its p-value.** The
surviving assist increments (1.3e-03 – 2.3e-03) are 6–15× the floor, so they are real; they are
simply not assist signals.

The iid-noise control reached correct-level p 0.0466 on exactly one of 20 cells — the expected
~1-in-20 — and died family-wise at fw_p 0.5574. The max-t correction is working.

## 7. WHAT THIS CLOSES, AND THE ONE LEAD LEFT

**Closes.** D051 was DISCHARGED for three targets and BLOCKED for two. The block was "no forecast
exists to characterise". This screen built honest baselines for both blocked targets and
characterised them. **D051 can be marked DISCHARGED for all five.** The programme's four-campaign
finding — that single-game player outcomes are dominated by an irreducible component and that
1 sd of any available signal cannot move a forecast far enough to matter — now covers rebounds and
assists as well as points and efficiency. It is a consolidating null, not a new frontier.

**The one lead left, stated so it is not lost.** Every assist candidate here is a *volume-weighted
aggregate* of teammates. A genuine playmaking channel would more plausibly live at **pair level** —
this player's historical assists *to* that specific teammate, weighted by whether that teammate is
available. That construction was not attempted, is not ruled out by anything measured here, and is
the only assist idea remaining that the negative sign does not already argue against. It would need
play-by-play assist attribution, which exists in `data/playbyplay/` and was not touched by this
screen.

**Not a lead.** More shot-location features for rebounds. Ten candidates across three targets, two
bases and two strata produced a best family-wise p of 0.3228. The upstream signal is real and well
evidenced (D074/D079/D087); it does not reach rebound counts, because rebound counts are dominated
by minutes and the player's own rate, both already in the reference.
