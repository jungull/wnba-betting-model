==================================================================================================
B. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR D103's PAIRED FAMILY
==================================================================================================
  D103 publishes finite mde80_fw for E1_I0023's 30 cells (median 0.0044) and counts 24 of
  them 'blind to 0.0023'.  The simulation above says the family-wise test on that design
  has NO finite MDE at all: those cells are blind to EVERY effect size, not merely to
  0.0023.  The published finite floor is not conservative -- it is not a floor.

  This moves D103's headline in the SAME direction it already points: 24 of the 30 were
  already counted blind, so the correction adds only the remaining 6 cells
  (760 -> 766 of 1349, 56.34%% -> 56.78%%).  The QUALITATIVE conclusion survives
  untouched; it was, if anything, slightly understated.

  IT DOES NOT VALIDATE E1_I0035's 6.6x.  The mechanism here is the critical value, not
  the variance, and the cell E1_I0035 named (488 player-season blocks, two-sided 1.96)
  is nowhere near the boundary -- sqrt(488)=22.1 against t_crit=1.96.

==================================================================================================
C. THE RESULT THAT MOST WEAKENS MY OWN CONCLUSION
==================================================================================================
  Everything above says the effect-carrying null_sd matters ONLY where the block count is
  small relative to t_crit^2.  Across the programme's actual cells that is rare:
     E1_I0035 player cells : 488 blocks, two-sided 1.96 -> H_B factor 1.005  (negligible)
     E1_I0035 team cells   :  36 blocks, two-sided 1.96 -> H_B factor 1.085  (small)
     E1_I0033 all cells    :  36 blocks, two-sided 1.96 -> H_B factor 1.085  (small)
     E1_I0023 per-cell     :  48 blocks, one-sided 1.645 -> H_B factor 1.045 (small)
     E1_I0023 family-wise  :  48 blocks, t_crit 6.974    -> H_B factor INFINITE
  So on the programme's own designs the defect I was sent to audit is NEGLIGIBLE almost
  everywhere, and catastrophic in exactly one place -- and that one place was already
  counted as blind. The headline number I was asked to check, 6.6x, does not survive at
  all: it is a comparison of two different response contrasts.
