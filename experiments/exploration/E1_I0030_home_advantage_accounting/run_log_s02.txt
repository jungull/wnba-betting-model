  *** NC2 ALSO FAILS, AND THE REASON IS DIAGNOSTIC RATHER THAN FATAL. ***
      team_id is not a meaningless label: it orders the franchises, and franchise
      identity encodes team STRENGTH.  Low ids in this partition are NYL/PHO/LVA/LAS,
      high ids are SEA/CHI/ATL, and those groups are not equally good.  Any FIXED
      team-level label therefore picks up a quality contrast and cannot be a null.
      This is exactly the confound that home/away does NOT have, and the reason is
      measurable: every team plays BOTH roles, so team strength cancels inside the
      home-minus-away contrast.  That balance is checked next rather than asserted.
