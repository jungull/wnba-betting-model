**The general lesson, which is bigger than this repair:** a diagnostic may legitimately read the
priced frame, because that is where the competitive question lives. An **arm may not**. Any
diagnostic finding that is to become a model change must first be re-derived using only what the
arm is allowed to read — otherwise the measured gain is partly a boundary violation, and it will
not survive implementation.
